**Role:** Co-Founder & Suspension System Chief
**Team:** Democritus Racing Team
**Domain:** Motorsports Engineering • Vehicle Dynamics • Structural Analysis
**Status:** Completed

---

##  Overview

As Co-Founder and Suspension Chief of the Democritus Racing Team, I led the design and development of the suspension system for a Formula SAE single-seat race car. The project required balancing vehicle dynamics performance, structural robustness, and manufacturability under tight competition constraints.

My responsibilities covered concept development, detailed design, simulation, testing, and team coordination.

---

##  Objectives

* Design a competitive Formula SAE suspension architecture
* Ensure structural integrity under racing loads
* Optimize weight and stiffness
* Enable manufacturable components
* Support vehicle handling performance
* Coordinate suspension subsystem development

---

##  Suspension Architecture


* Double wishbone configuration
* Pushrod actuation 
* Coilover damper integration
* Adjustable geometry for tuning
